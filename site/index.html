How the labs teach

Designed for high-school and early-undergraduate students, and for anyone curious. Algebra and basic physics are enough; deeper maths is there when you want it.

?

Predict, then run

Before each key run you commit to a prediction. The result appears next to your guess, and the vocabulary comes after the surprise, not before it.

≡

Three depth levels

Explore shows only the lab. Understand adds the Bloch sphere and probabilities. Math adds state vectors, matrices and the equations behind every plot.

⟩

Hardware and circuit, side by side

Every laser pulse is also a gate. The circuit diagram is drawn from the same pulse list the lab animates, so the two can never disagree.
